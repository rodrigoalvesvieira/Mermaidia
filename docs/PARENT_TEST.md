# Optional supervised usability check — not yet performed

Use local keyboard/touch play. Do not record a child or enable child cloud audio; deployment prerequisites are unconfirmed. Ask a consenting parent to supervise and stop whenever the child wishes. Let the parent handle the initial local microphone-permission prompt: the permission probe closes its track immediately and does not transmit to a provider; denial leaves keyboard/touch play available.

1. Show the ocean map. Observe whether the child chooses a destination and one starting spot without reading assistance.
2. Ask them to swim toward something interesting, turn, glide sideways, and stop. Note confusing icons or overshooting without coaching immediately.
3. Invite them to press the golden question mark. Observe whether they understand the spoken fact and close the card.
4. Invite them to visit the surface, then return underwater. Check comfort with the camera and transitions.
5. Open the journal, choose a remembered creature, and return to the map. Explain that leaving is always fine.

For a later approved child-voice deployment, repeat only after actual provider retention controls are verified. No recording is necessary. A parent may note whether “stop”, “turn right”, “go right”, and “go to the surface” behaved as expected, and whether the voice understood the child. Adult/synthetic fixtures do not establish five-year-old recognition accuracy.

Record only anonymous task-level observations with parent consent: completed independently / needed help / confusing, plus visual or audio discomfort. Do not enter names, exact ages, transcripts, recordings, or identifying details into the application or repository.
